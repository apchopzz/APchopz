Cancellation Policy
Last updated: April 2026

Out of respect for everyone's time, please read the following policy before booking. By scheduling an appointment with APchopz, you agree to these terms.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

No charge
24+ Hours

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment and there's no issue — just reach out and we'll sort a new time.

Late notice
Under 24 Hours

Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may result in a fee for future bookings. Life happens — just communicate early.

No-Shows

If you don't show up and don't reach out, that appointment slot is gone and can't be given to someone else. After a no-show:

  • You'll be required to prepay before booking future appointments
  • Repeat no-shows may result in being unable to book

Late Arrivals

If you're running late, just text me. I'll do my best to accommodate you. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may result in a shortened service or rescheduling, depending on the schedule that day.

Satisfaction

If you're not happy with your cut, speak up before you leave the chair. I'll do everything I can to make it right on the spot. Refunds are not offered once you've left the studio, but your satisfaction matters — so let's fix it in person.

How to Cancel or Reschedule

The easiest way is to text me directly. You can also reach out on Instagram. Please don't just not show up — a quick message goes a long way.
