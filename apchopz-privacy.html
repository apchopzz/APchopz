Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 2026

Who We Are

APchopz Barber Studio is a personal barbershop business operated by Alex Poitevien. This privacy policy explains how we handle the information you provide when booking an appointment through our website.

What Information We Collect

When you book an appointment, we collect the following:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Your preferred service, date, and time
  • Any notes or style references you provide

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Confirm and manage your appointment
  • Contact you about scheduling changes or cancellations
  • Provide the barber service you requested

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store Your Information

Appointment details are stored in Google Calendar, which is protected by Google's security infrastructure. We do not maintain a separate database of client information. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request that your information be deleted at any time
  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Opt out of any future communication

To make any of these requests, contact us directly using the information below.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any tracking technology. We do not run ads or use analytics tools that track your behavior.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.
